Guiying Yan is a Professor at the Academy of Mathematics and Systems Science, Chinese Academy of Sciences (CAS), Deputy Director of the National Center for Mathematics and Interdisciplinary Sciences, CAS, and a Professor at the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences. She serves as Secretary of the Party Committee, Vice President, and Secretary-General of the Operations Research Society of China. Her research focuses on graph and network theory and its applications. She has led projects supported by the National Key R&D Program of China and the Key Program of the National Natural Science Foundation of China, as well as several industry collaboration projects. She has been recognized as a National Outstanding Scientific and Technological Worker, receives the State Council&#39;s Special Government Allowance, and has received multiple provincial and ministerial awards.
