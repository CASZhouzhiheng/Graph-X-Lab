I am currently a Postdoctoral Researcher at the School of Mathematics and Statistics, Shandong University, working with Prof. Xingqin Qi. Previously, I was a Postdoctoral Researcher at the Academy of Mathematics and Systems Science, Chinese Academy of Sciences (AMSS, CAS), working with Prof. Fuzhou Gong. I received my Ph.D. in Operations Research and Cybernetics from AMSS, CAS, in 2023, under the supervision of Prof. Guiying Yan, with whom I continue to collaborate closely. My research focuses on graph and hypergraph learning, with particular interests in neural diffusion models, higher-order interactions, and trustworthy graph neural networks. I also investigate their applications to bioinformatics and biomedical data analysis, including brain disorder diagnosis and drug combination prediction. My work has appeared in journals and conferences including TPAMI, MedIA, JBHI, KBS, KDD, CIKM.
