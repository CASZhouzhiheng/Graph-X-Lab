Qi Wang is a lecturer and master's supervisor at the College of Science, China Agricultural University, which he joined in September 2023. He received his PhD from the Academy of Mathematics and Systems Science, Chinese Academy of Sciences, in 2021, under the supervision of Professor Guiying Yan. From 2021 to 2023, he conducted postdoctoral research at the Institute of Biophysics, Chinese Academy of Sciences, under the mentorship of Professor Ang Li. He was selected for the Young Talent Support Program of the Beijing Interdisciplinary Science Society. His research focuses on graph computing and AI for Science. His work has appeared in journals including Nature Methods, Nature Human Behaviour, and IEEE Journal of Biomedical and Health Informatics.
